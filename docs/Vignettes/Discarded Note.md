# Discarded Note
written by Steven, found by Cobalt Wilton on a run

---
*(The handwriting is rough, but readable.)*

Steven, Thirauftkni, Branch 5, District 7, Pack 3

I haven't told anyone, but a firauftkni named Jade has been teaching me how to write. I like Jade, she's nice. Helped me up one day when I was in a rough shape. Jade says writing my thoughts will help me think. Pack leader says not to talk to the other kni other than thirauftkni. We're all afraid of him. They're just as kni as we are though. So doing it anyway when off duty. Can't tell me what to do when I'm off duty.

I honestly forget what we're fighting for. We have the city, humans are gone, why still fight? Was I told? Took Crystallyne 5 years ago but don't know why. It's a nice city though. Wish people weren't as afraid of us but I guess I can't blame them. Wish they knew that we're afraid of our higher ups too. One pack was claimed by higher ups because they let a sirauftkni runner get away while on duty. Go against higher ups and you get put down. Always been like that, even in the tribes.

Jade says to not let higher ups see writings. She says would be dangerous for her and me. I will try my best to keep-

*(The rest of the page is burned and unreadable.)*
