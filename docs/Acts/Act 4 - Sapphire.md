### Sapphire's journal, entry 3

I haven't sewn at all yet today...

I found an old, red lock box while poking around in the ruins of our house.

He had given me a key, but he never told me what was inside it. He only said it was "for when we were older and needed something to laugh at." I had thought that it burned in the blackfires, but it looks hardly even melted, just covered in black ash.

I'm... not sure I was ready for this.

It's our letters. From when I was a girl.

Our focus school was running a program were we would become pen pals with the students of a different school. I was assigned a boy named Steel. We chatted somewhat, and he asked if I had any interest in fantasy stories.

I'd...experimented in the past, but I don't think it was ever very good. I said as much. He proposed that we build a story one letter at a time, rolling dice to see if each other's actions succeeded each time. His character was Sir George Lance of Synehall, and I was Sarai Baker, daughter of the village baker. Sarai had always dreamed of adventure.

She definitely found it.

With a man named Steel.
