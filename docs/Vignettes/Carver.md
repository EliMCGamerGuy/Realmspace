# Carver
recorded by Zinc Lithium

---

\[5AU, The Resistance Surveillance room\]
Shelia: "Oh, Zinc."
Zinc: "Hmm?"
Shelia: "I found a cassette tape on the street near the recent casualty."
Zinc: "Oh? Let's hear it."
*(Shelia passes Zinc an ashy cassette. Zinc sighs, and dusts it off. He places it in his tape deck.)*

"My name is Phillip Carver. Humanlike kni. Most kni's focus weapons are traditional. Shortswords, longswords, scimitars... I saw an axe one time."

"Mine's a knife."

"Up until now, I've just used it as a tool. Nothing fancy. Dad always wanted me to take up the family business with it, but I'm going to be honest, I was never interested."

"Found a new use for it. Started taking them out a week ago. One by one, night by night. A replica gets isolated, then it's gone. Nobody misses them, right?"

"You gotta wait until they're in the dark areas. Where nobody can see them. Strike fast and strike hard so they can't call the others."

"I'm gonna try to get that larger one tonight. See how it goes. Who knows, it might work out."

*(Zinc stops the tape.)*

...

Shelia: "Hey, Zinc...could you pull up the recording from when that casualty was claimed?"
*(Zinc opens the video, the system patches it through to the recording as well.)*

*(It's night. There's a lone replica kni standing by the side of the road.)*

*(A figure creeps up behind them.)*

*(The figure quickly grabs the replica kni from behind and, with a focus knife, slits their neck. The replica grabs the figure and pulls them up over themselves, slamming them onto the ground. They then focus their sword and thrust it into the other kni's chest, and fall beside them clutching their neck shortly after.)*

...

Zinc: "Yeah...that lines up."
*(Zinc sighs.)*
"It's reckless."

...

Shelia: "You know, it's easy to forget they're people too. They have personalities, families..."
Zinc: "A lot of them don't keep their families."
Shelia: "There's a whole branch that do."

...

"Not all of them are bad. Some of them lobby the higher ups for better treatment of other kni."

"I wonder what it would be like if circumstances were different."

Zinc: "You're talking about only roughly a tenth of them."

"But it is an interesting idea."

"Just please be careful who you share it with, Shel. We may've killed thousands of them but they've killed hundreds of thousands of us, counting humans."

"And some people won't take too kindly to you thinking about them that way."
